# 🎯 Careera AI

### AI-powered career and job application platform

Before building Webprowale, I built **Careera AI**, a functional AI career platform that is actively used by job seekers.

I built Careera AI to solve a problem I saw in the modern job-search process:

> **Job seekers spend enormous amounts of time applying for jobs without knowing how well they actually match the role, whether their resume is optimized for the position, what skills they are missing, or how to present their experience effectively.**

Traditional job applications often require candidates to repeatedly rewrite resumes, create cover letters, analyze job descriptions, prepare for interviews, and track applications across different tools.

I wanted to build a single AI-powered system that could help with the **entire job application workflow**.

### What Careera AI solves

Careera AI helps job seekers turn a generic job search into a more **targeted, data-driven application process**.

Instead of simply asking an AI chatbot to "improve my resume," users can work with their actual resume and target job to understand:

* How well their resume matches a specific role
* Which skills they already have
* Which skills they are missing
* Which keywords are missing from their resume
* How their experience aligns with the position
* How to improve their resume for the specific job
* How to create a relevant cover letter
* How to prepare for interviews
* How to organize and track applications

### Core AI Features

* 📄 **AI Resume Analysis** — analyzes resumes for ATS readiness, keywords, structure, formatting, and impact.
* 🎯 **AI Job Matching** — compares a resume against a job description and provides a compatibility score and detailed breakdown.
* 🧠 **Skill Gap Analysis** — identifies missing skills and prioritizes what the candidate should improve.
* ✍️ **Resume Tailoring** — adapts a resume to a specific job while keeping the candidate's real experience.
* 💼 **Cover Letter Generation** — creates personalized cover letters based on the candidate and target role.
* 📚 **Learning Paths** — creates personalized development plans around career goals and skill gaps.
* 📊 **Application Tracking** — helps users organize applications, interviews, and follow-ups.
* 🎙️ **AI Interviewer** — provides role-specific interview practice with real-time voice interaction and dynamic follow-up questions.

Careera AI is designed around the idea that **AI should understand the candidate, understand the opportunity, and help connect the two.**
